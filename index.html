About the game

Rocket League was released in July 2015 by Psyonix. Two teams of cars try to hit a ball into the other team's goal. Matches last five minutes, and the most common gamemode is 3v3.

It grew out of an earlier game, Supersonic Acrobatic Rocket-Powered Battle-Cars, and became free to play in September 2020 when it was put onto the epic games store.

Mechanics I love

Boost

Collect boost pads around the pitch to fuel your car. The best players always know which pads they will grab next.

Aerials

Jump, boost and tilt your car back to hit the ball while it is in the air. It takes lots of practise, but using air-roll you can manoeuvre your car.

Flip resets

Touch the ball with all four of your wheels in mid-air to get your flip back. It is one of the hardest tricks in the game.

The arena

Quick facts

  • Standard mode: 3v3
  • Match length: 5 minutes
  • Multiple different gamemodes, ranging from 1v1, 2v2, and 3v3
  • A popular esport surrounding the game, with the largest competition being the RLCS
  • Ranks run from Bronze up to Supersonic Legend
  • Played on PC, console and mobile

Why I play

Every match is different. Games are short, so it is easy to fit one in, and there is always something new to practise. With so many mechanics, constantly evolving the game. Playing with friends and pulling off a good passing play together is the best part.
